About Faros Incepta

Strategy meets business insight.

Faros Incepta is an Illinois company that helps businesses and organizations grow online. We build websites, run Meta advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and create ready-made audio clips that give brands a voice.

Faros is Greek for lighthouse. That is our job: to make you easy to find and to guide the right people to you. Our approach rests on a simple idea: your online presence should do more than exist. It should help move the business forward.
